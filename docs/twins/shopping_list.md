# Physical-twin shopping list

15 kinds of item, 23 pieces in all, one supermarket trip. Each stands in for one 3D asset. Sizes are the asset's real size; anything within a fifth either way is fine. Pick plain items close to the colour given, because questions name objects by colour.

1. **Apples x2** (red): large red apples; about 9.8 cm across and 8.5 cm tall (fine from 7.8 to 11.7 cm across, 6.8 to 10.2 cm tall). Asset `food_apple_01`.
2. **Pomegranate x1** (red): a pomegranate; about 11.5 cm across (fine from 9.2 to 13.8 cm). Asset `food_pomegranate_01`.
3. **Lemons x3** (yellow): lemons; about 6.9 cm across and 9.5 cm long (fine from 5.5 to 8.2 cm across, 7.6 to 11.4 cm long). Asset `lemon`.
4. **Limes x3** (green): limes; about 6.1 cm across and 7.5 cm long (fine from 4.9 to 7.4 cm across, 6 to 9 cm long). Asset `food_lime_01`.
5. **Avocado x1** (green): a large avocado; about 9.5 cm across and 14.3 cm long (fine from 7.6 to 11.4 cm across, 11.5 to 17.2 cm long). Asset `food_avocado_01`.
6. **Kiwis x2** (brown): large kiwi fruit; about 7 cm across and 9 cm long (fine from 5.6 to 8.3 cm across, 7.2 to 10.8 cm long). Asset `food_kiwi_01`.
7. **Onions x2** (brown): brown onions; about 7.2 cm across and 8.6 cm long (fine from 5.8 to 8.7 cm across, 6.9 to 10.4 cm long). Asset `yellow_onion`.
8. **Small bowl x1** (brown): a small bowl, wooden if possible; about 13.1 cm across and 7.3 cm tall (fine from 10.5 to 15.7 cm across, 5.8 to 8.8 cm tall). Asset `wooden_bowl_02`.

12. **Spray bottle x1** (grey): a grey or clear trigger spray bottle (kitchen cleaner); about 9 cm across and 22.9 cm tall (fine from 7.2 to 10.8 cm across, 18.3 to 27.4 cm tall). Asset `multi_cleaner_bottle`.
13. **Bleach bottle x1** (white): a white bleach or detergent bottle; about 29.4 cm tall on a 8.8 x 6.1 cm footprint (fine from 23.5 to 35.2 cm tall, each side within a fifth). Asset `bleach_bottle`.
14. **Tape roll x1** (white): a small white roll of medical or masking tape; about 4.5 cm across and 2.5 cm wide (fine from 3.6 to 5.4 cm across, 2 to 3 cm wide). Asset `medical_tape`.
15. **Rubber duck x1** (yellow): a yellow rubber duck (baby aisle); about 11.4 cm tall on a 12 x 8.5 cm footprint (fine from 9.1 to 13.7 cm tall, each side within a fifth). Asset `rubber_duck_toy`.

Also: the printed mat (docs/twins/mat_A3.pdf, or the four A4 tiles of mat_A4_tiled.pdf), sticky tape, and a phone stand or a stack of books for the camera spot.

Not on the list: trays, plates, baskets and the big bowl are wider than the mat's placement area is deep (19 cm); the wooden spoon and the croissant are too long.
