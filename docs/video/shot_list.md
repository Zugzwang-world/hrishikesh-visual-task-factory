## Set up the desk once

1. The printed A3 mat (docs/twins/mat_A3.pdf at 100%) flat on the desk, all four black corner squares visible.
2. Layout H1 on the mat (docs/twins/hero_layouts.pdf page 2), each object's middle on its spot, within half a centimetre. Positions in cm from the middle of the mat (x to the right, y toward the back edge):

   - white tape at (+12.6, -4.4)
   - white bottle at (-8.5, +3.9)
   - brown onion at (-11.1, -5.7)
   - brown kiwi at (+5.0, +4.2)
   - green lime at (+2.6, -5.5)

3. The phone that plays the robot camera: the front spot of the photo guide: straight in front of the mat, in line with its centre, about 28 cm in front of the mat's front edge, lens about 50 cm above the table on a stack of books, tilted down about 50 degrees so the middle of the mat is in the middle of the screen. This is the pose the H1 photos are taken from, so the 3D twin in the Explorer lines up with the shot.
4. The recording camera (a second phone, or the Mac with QuickTime, New Movie Recording): landscape, 1080p, 30 fps, at eye level about 1.2 to 1.5 m away, so the founder, the mat and the phone on its books are all in frame. A quiet room; the phone's microphone within a metre, or wired earphones with a microphone.
5. Take the H1 photos for the photo session first. D2 moves the tape.
